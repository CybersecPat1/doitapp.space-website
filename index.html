Privacy Policy

No data is collected by the application.

All AI processing is performed on your device using local Apple Intelligence models. Your content is not sent to an external AI service for processing.

When you use connected calendars or RSS feeds, the application communicates with those services to retrieve the content you request. Those services are subject to their own privacy policies.
